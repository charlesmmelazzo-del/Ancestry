Mary Fanny Lawson Winstead

fl. early 1800s

Mother of Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton. Maiden name Lawson.

Documented

Mary Fanny Lawson Winstead (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ John Wilson Winstead │ ▼ Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton (1841–1926)

Mary Fanny Lawson married John Wilson Winstead in early nineteenth-century Hawkins County, Tennessee. Together they raised at least eleven children, including Nancy Jane (born 1841), who would marry Robert Thomas Stapleton. The Lawson surname is English/Scots-Irish, common in the southern Appalachians.
