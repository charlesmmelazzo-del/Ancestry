Quesenberry Line — Generation 5

Crockett Quisenberry

1822–1902

Lived from Andrew Jackson's first term to the dawn of Theodore Roosevelt's century. Saw the Civil War take his son.

Partially documented

James S. Quesenberry (1796–1845) Elizabeth Moore Quesenberry (1800–1850) │ ▼ Crockett Quisenberry (1822–1902) ⚭ Nancy J. Quesenberry │ ▼ Pvt. Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Sr. (1842–1863)

Crockett Quisenberry was born in 1822, in the year of Andrew Jackson's first presidential campaign. His given name Crockett was almost certainly inspired by Davy Crockett, the famous Tennessee frontiersman and politician who was at the height of his national fame in those years.

Crockett lived eighty years, dying in 1902. He saw the Civil War take his son A. (born 1842, died 1863) — a wound a parent never recovers from. He lived to see his grandson Alexander grow to adulthood and his great-grandson Floyd Harmon Quesenberry be born in 1885.
