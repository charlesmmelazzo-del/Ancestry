Harvey Line — Generation 9

Nicholas Hutchins

fl. late 1600s – early 1700s

Father of Catherine Hutchins Stanley, patriarch of the Hutchins line in colonial Henrico, Virginia.

Partially documented

Nicholas Hutchins (fl. late 1600s – early 1700s) ⚭ Mary Watkins Hutchins │ ▼ Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley (1703–1766)

Nicholas Hutchins lived in colonial Henrico, Virginia in the late seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries. He married Mary Watkins and together they raised a large family of at least eleven children — Strangeman, Martha, Mary, James, Carrie, John, Sarah, Benjamin, Elizabeth, Alice, and Catherine "Rachel" (Mike's gen-8 ancestor, born ~1703). The Hutchins family was one of the foundational families of Henrico County, Virginia — a county established in 1634 as one of the eight original shires of colonial Virginia. Nicholas's own birth and death dates have not yet been recovered.
