Mary Watkins Hutchins

fl. late 1600s – early 1700s

Mother of Catherine Hutchins Stanley. Of the Watkins family of colonial Henrico, Virginia.

Partially documented

Mary Watkins Hutchins (fl. late 1600s – early 1700s) ⚭ Nicholas Hutchins │ ▼ Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley (1703–1766)

Mary Watkins married Nicholas Hutchins in colonial Henrico, Virginia, in the late seventeenth century. Together they raised at least eleven Hutchins children, including Catherine "Rachel" (Mike's gen-8 direct ancestor), who would later marry James Stanley Sr. and migrate with him south to Guilford County, North Carolina. The Watkins surname is English, common in colonial Virginia from the founding era forward.
