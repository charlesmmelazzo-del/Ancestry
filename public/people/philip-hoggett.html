Harvey Line — Generation 8

Philip Hoggett

fl. early 1700s

Husband of Mary Hoggatt. Colonial Quaker patriarch of this Hoggatt-Harvey line.

Partially documented

Philip Hoggett — colonial-era husband of Mary Hoggatt. The slight spelling difference (Hoggett vs Hoggatt) is typical of eighteenth-century records where surnames were not yet standardized. From this couple descend the Hoggatts who would join the Quaker migration south to the Carolina Piedmont, eventually merging into the Harvey line.
