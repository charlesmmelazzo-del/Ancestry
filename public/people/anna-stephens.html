Anna Stephens McMichael

1818–1899

Born Guilford NC 14 November 1818. Married Jesse C. McMichael 4 September 1842. Bore nine children, lived 80 years in the same Deep River community.

Documented

Anna Stephens McMichael (1818–1899) ⚭ Jesse C. McMichael │ ▼ William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941)

Anna Stephens was born on the 14th of November, 1818, in Guilford County, North Carolina. The Stephens surname is English, well-attested in Piedmont North Carolina from the colonial period — her own parents have not yet been recovered.

On the 4th of September, 1842, in Guilford County, she married Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907). She was 23; he was 22. Over the next two decades they raised nine children — Sarah J. (later Highfill), George A., Rhoda Melissa (later Rayle), William Franklin (Mike's gen-3 ancestor, who would father Geneva), John W., Washington C., Mary, Martha (later Parish), and Jesse A. The 1850 federal census places the household in Guilford County; the 1880 census places them at Bruce, Guilford County — both within the broader Deep River community.

Anna died on the 4th of August, 1899, age 80 — outliving five of America's presidents she was born under. Her husband Jesse would follow her eight years later in 1907.
