Harvey Line — Generation 7

Mary Agnes Stanley Hoggatt

1733–1808

Born MARY AGNES STANLEY in Hanover County VA — not a Hoggatt by birth, as her name in our tree might suggest. She married Anthony P. Hoggatt and took his surname.

Documented

James Stanley, Sr. (fl. early-mid 1700s) Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley (1703–1766) │ ▼ Mary Agnes Stanley Hoggatt (1733–1808) ⚭ Anthony P. Hoggatt

Mary Agnes Stanley Hoggatt was born on the 27th of June, 1733, at Cedar Creek, Hanover County, Virginia — to James Stanley, Sr. and Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley (~1703–1766). She was the fourth of at least eight Stanley children. The Stanleys were a Virginia-then-North-Carolina Quaker family, and Catherine herself was a Hutchins of Henrico, Virginia by birth.

Mary Agnes married Anthony P. Hoggatt (1730–1814) — and the Stanley line merged into the Hoggatt line. Together they raised the next generation of Hoggatts in the Virginia and Carolina Quaker communities. The earlier surname 'Hoggatt' in this entry was Mary Agnes's married name only; her birth surname was Stanley. (The renaming reflects what we learned in May 2026 via MyHeritage record-match deep dive.)

She died on the 21st of January, 1808, at Yadkin, in Stokes County, North Carolina — having migrated south from her birthplace some time in the late eighteenth century, part of the broader Quaker movement out of Virginia and into the Piedmont Carolina settlements. Through her, the Hutchins-Stanley lineage joined the Hoggatt-Glendenning-Newlin-Harvey lineage, all of them Quaker, all of them eventually arriving in Guilford County, NC.
