Isaac Trueblood

fl. late 1700s – early 1800s

Father of Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey. Of the colonial Quaker Trueblood family.

Partially documented

Isaac Trueblood (fl. late 1700s – early 1800s) ⚭ Mary Outland Trueblood │ ▼ Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey (1812–before 1870)

Isaac Trueblood — father of Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey (Mike's gen-4 ancestor) and patriarch of this Trueblood line in Guilford County, NC. He married Mary Outland (whose own surname is itself a colonial Quaker name) and together they raised at least nine children: Martha, Jonathan J., Isabella (later Bradshaw), Mary Outland (named for her mother), Rachel, Milicent, Jesse B., Thomas, and Sarah Sally, born 21 September 1812, who would marry Anthony Harvey. The Trueblood surname is famously associated with the early Quakers of Perquimans and Pasquotank Counties on North Carolina's Albemarle Sound — established there in the 1670s. By Isaac's generation the Trueblood family had spread inland to the Piedmont counties including Guilford.
