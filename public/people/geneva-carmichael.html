Geneva Esther McMicheal Harvey

1891–1971

Geneva Elizabeth — daughter of William Franklin McMichael of Deep River, Guilford County. Lost her mother Sally Pegram McMichael in early childhood (sometime between 1891–1897). Married William Carl Harvey on a June day in 1919.

Geneva Esther McMicheal was born on the 5th of September, 1891, in North Carolina. Earlier research called her maiden name Carmichael; the documentary record corrects that — her maiden name was McMichael (occasionally spelled McMicheal). The error matters: McMichael and Carmichael are distinct Scottish-derived surnames, and the entire Highland Scotland / Jupiter of Larne migration story we once told about this branch does not apply to your family. The McMichaels of Guilford County are their own line, with their own American story.

Her father was William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941), of the Deep River community in Guilford County, North Carolina. Her mother was named Sally Pegram (born about 1860), though Find a Grave alternately identifies her mother as Esther Jane McMichael — a discrepancy worth resolving (Geneva's middle name Esther may well honor her mother). The 1900 federal census records her, age 8, in the household at Deep River.

On the 20th of June, 1919, she married William Carl Harvey in Guilford County. She was twenty-seven; he was thirty-two. They settled in Deep River, where the 1930 and 1950 censuses found them with their two daughters Ruth and Esther. Her father died on 3 April 1941 at Forsyth, NC, age 91 or 92.

Geneva outlived her husband by five years. She died on the 27th of February, 1971, at Winston-Salem in Forsyth County, age 79, and was buried in Colfax, Guilford County — where her daughter Esther would be buried fifty years later, at Shady Grove Wesleyan Church Cemetery.
