Annis Stapleton Mullins

fl. late 1800s

Mother of Eva Elizabeth Mullins. Daughter of Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911) and Nancy Jane Winstead (b. 1841).

Documented

Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911) Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton (1841–1926) │ ▼ Annis Stapleton Mullins (fl. late 1800s) ⚭ James Calloway Mullins │ ▼ Eva Elizabeth Mullins Quesenberry (1890–1983)

Annis Stapleton was a daughter of Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911) and Nancy Jane Winstead (b. 1841) — both of the Piedmont American Stapleton-Winstead families. She married James C. C. Mullins (1854–1929) and bore Eva Elizabeth Mullins (1891–1983), who would become Lonnie's mother.
