Lucretia Juanita Fuller Pegram

fl. early 1800s

Mother of James Fletcher Pegram. Maiden name Fuller — an English Piedmont surname.

Partially documented

Lucretia Juanita Fuller Pegram (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Jesse Pegram │ ▼ James Fletcher Pegram (1831–1915)

Lucretia Juanita Fuller — sometimes recorded as Loucretia on her son's death certificate — was the mother of James Fletcher Pegram. The Fuller surname is English, well-attested in colonial and antebellum North Carolina. She raised her son in the Pegram community of Rockingham County, North Carolina; he would later move to Deep River Township, Guilford County, where her great-granddaughter Geneva would be born in 1891.
