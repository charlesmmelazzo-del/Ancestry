Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram

1826–1917

Born Rockingham County NC 19 January 1826. Married James Fletcher Pegram 1853. Outlived her husband by two years, dying at 91 in Deep River.

Documented

Charles O. Gates (fl. early 1800s) Nancy D. Mustin Gates (fl. early 1800s) │ ▼ Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram (1826–1917) ⚭ James Fletcher Pegram │ ▼ Sally Pegram McMichael (1861–c.1895)

Annie Elizabeth Gates was born on the 19th of January, 1826, in Rockingham County, North Carolina — daughter of Charles O. Gates and Nancy D. Mustin Gates (sometimes spelled Mustin/Musten on records). She grew up with at least four siblings: Sarah L., Mary A., James, and Wesley. The Gates surname is English, well-attested in Rockingham County from the early nineteenth century.

On the 10th of March, 1853, in Forsyth County, NC, Annie married James Fletcher Pegram — making her five years his senior, unusual but not unheard of in 19th-century rural America. Together they raised five children: William Henry, Mary D., John C., Sally (Geneva's mother), and Nancy E. Pegram. The household moved with James from Rockingham to Deep River Township, Guilford County, where they would remain for the rest of their lives.

Annie outlived her husband James (who died June 1915) by nearly two years. She died on the 13th of March, 1917, in Deep River, age 91 — and was buried two days later. By that point her daughter Sally had been dead for at least twenty years, and Sally's daughter Geneva (Annie's granddaughter) was 25 years old, raising Ruth's mother in the next generation.
