Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram

1826–?

Wife of James Fletcher Pegram, mother of Sally. Five years older than her husband — unusual for the era.

Documented

Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram (1826–?) ⚭ James Fletcher Pegram │ ▼ Sally Pegram McMichael (1860–?)

Annie Elizabeth Gates was born in 1826. She married James Fletcher Pegram (born 1831) — making her five years his senior, which was unusual in nineteenth-century rural America. Together they raised Sally Pegram, who would marry William Franklin McMichael.
