William Franklin McMichael

1849–1941

Father of Geneva — born in 1849 (the year of the California Gold Rush), died in April 1941 at ninety-one or ninety-two, just months before Pearl Harbor. Son of Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907).

Documented

Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907) Anna Stephens McMichael (1818–1899) │ ▼ William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941) ⚭ Sally Pegram McMichael │ ▼ Geneva Esther McMicheal Harvey (1891–1971)

William Franklin McMichael was born in 1849 — the year of the California Gold Rush — to Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907) and Anna Stephens. The McMichael surname is Scots-Irish (Ulster-Scot), well-attested in Piedmont North Carolina from the eighteenth century onward.

He lived in Deep River, Guilford County, North Carolina — the same farming community where his daughter Geneva would later raise her own family. He married Sally Pegram (born about 1860, daughter of James Fletcher Pegram and Annie Elizabeth Gates); together they had at least one daughter, Geneva Esther, in 1891.

He lived to ninety-one or ninety-two — a remarkable span for his generation. He died on the 3rd of April, 1941, in Forsyth County, NC, just eight months before the United States entered the Second World War. His daughter Geneva was forty-nine when she lost him.
