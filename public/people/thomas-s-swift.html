Thomas S. Swift (fl. early-mid 1800s) ⚭ Lucinda Cox Swift │ ▼ Rachel Frances Swift Harvey (fl. mid-1800s)

Thomas S. Swift — father of Rachel Frances Swift Harvey. He married Lucinda Cox (1822–1889) of the Cox family of Piedmont North Carolina. Together they raised Rachel, who would marry Isaac J. Harvey and bear William Carl Harvey — Ruth's father.
