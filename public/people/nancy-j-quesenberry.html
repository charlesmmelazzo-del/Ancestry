Nancy J. Quesenberry

1820–1901

Wife of Crockett Quisenberry. Her surname Quesenberry suggests she may have been a cousin — common in the heavily intermarried Floyd County hollows.

Partially documented

Nancy J. Quesenberry (1820–1901) ⚭ Crockett Quisenberry │ ▼ Pvt. Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Sr. (1842–1863)

Nancy J. Quesenberry was born in 1820. Her surname Quesenberry — also her husband's — strongly suggests they were cousins, perhaps first or second. In the Quesenberry settlements of Floyd County, Virginia in the early nineteenth century, this was unremarkable: most Quesenberrys married Quesenberrys, Hyltons, or Slushers. She lived eighty-one years, dying in 1901.
