Manerva Jane Mullins

1831–1910

Of the Claiborne County, Tennessee Mullins families. Mother of James Calloway Mullins.

Documented

Manerva Jane Mullins (1831–1910) ⚭ Harrison Mullins │ ▼ James Calloway Mullins (1854–1929)

Manerva Jane Mullins was born in January 1831 in Claiborne County, Tennessee — the heart of the Tennessee Mullins country, where the Cumberland Gap brings Virginia, Tennessee, and Kentucky together. She married Harrison Mullins (almost certainly her cousin, given their shared surname) and bore James Calloway Mullins (1854–1929), Eva's father. She lived to be 79, dying in 1910 in Lee County, Virginia — having crossed the state line from Tennessee in her later years.
