William HARVEY (colonial)

fl. late 1600s – early 1700s

Colonial-era Harvey patriarch. Married Judith ca. 1715; from him descend the American Harveys of this line.

Partially documented

William HARVEY — the colonial-era patriarch of this Harvey line. He married Judith around 1715 and from them every Harvey on this site descends. The capitalization of his surname in tree-of-record sources suggests he was a recorded landholder of the early eighteenth century, likely in Virginia or North Carolina.
