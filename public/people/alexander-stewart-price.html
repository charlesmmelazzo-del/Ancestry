Alexander Stewart Price

1833–1909

Father of Matilda Jane Price, grandfather of Floyd Harmon Quesenberry. Lived seventy-six years through the Civil War, Reconstruction, and the Wright Brothers' first flight.

Documented

Alexander Stewart Price (1833–1909) ⚭ Martha Jane Collins Price │ ▼ Matilda Jane Price Quesenberry (1867–1944)

Alexander Stewart Price was born in 1833 — the year Andrew Jackson began his second term. He married Martha Jane Collins and together they raised Matilda Jane Price (1867–1944), who would marry Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr. and become Floyd Harmon Quesenberry's mother. Alexander Stewart lived to be seventy-six, dying in 1909.
