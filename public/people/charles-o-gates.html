Charles O. Gates

fl. early 1800s

Father of Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram. Of the Gates family of Rockingham County, NC.

Documented

Charles O. Gates (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Nancy D. Mustin Gates │ ▼ Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram (1826–1917)

Charles O. Gates lived in Rockingham County, North Carolina in the early nineteenth century. He married Nancy D. Mustin and together they raised at least five children: Sarah L., Mary A., James, Wesley, and Annie Elizabeth (born 19 January 1826), who would marry James Fletcher Pegram in 1853 and become Geneva's grandmother through her daughter Sally. The Gates surname is English, common in colonial and antebellum North Carolina.
