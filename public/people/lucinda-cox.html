Lucinda Cox Swift

1822–1889

Wife of Thomas S. Swift, mother of Rachel. Born under James Monroe, died at sixty-seven under Benjamin Harrison.

Documented

Lucinda Cox Swift (1822–1889) ⚭ Thomas S. Swift │ ▼ Rachel Frances Swift Harvey (fl. mid-1800s)

Lucinda Cox was born in 1822, in the year of Andrew Jackson's first presidential campaign. She married Thomas S. Swift of the Piedmont North Carolina Swift family and bore Rachel Frances Swift, who would marry Isaac J. Harvey. The Cox surname is English, very common in Piedmont North Carolina from the eighteenth century forward — and has connections (through Floyd County, Virginia branches) to the Quesenberry-Cox families of southwestern Virginia. She died in 1889, age 67.
