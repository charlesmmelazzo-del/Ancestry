Agnes Harvey

1756–1834

Born Christmas Day 1756 in Rowan County, NC. Buried at Spring Friends Burial Grounds — a confirmed Quaker resting place.

Documented

Agnes Harvey (1756–1834) ⚭ Nathan Heaver Harvey │ ▼ Anthony Harvey (1791–1846)

Agnes Harvey was born on the 25th of December, 1756 — Christmas Day — in Rowan County, North Carolina. She was three years older than her husband Nathan Heaver Harvey, whom she married before 1791 (the birth year of their son Anthony).

She is buried at the Spring Friends Burial Grounds in Orange County, NC — a Quaker cemetery that confirms her religious affiliation. The Spring Friends Meeting was one of the earliest Quaker meetings established in Piedmont North Carolina by the migration from Pennsylvania.

She lived to be 77, dying on the 25th of May, 1834, three years before her husband.
