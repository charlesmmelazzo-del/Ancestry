James Franklin Stapleton

fl. early 1800s

Father of Robert Thomas Stapleton. Of the Stapleton family of Hawkins County, Tennessee.

Documented

James Franklin Stapleton (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Susan Singleton Stapleton │ ▼ Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911)

James Franklin Stapleton was the father of Robert Thomas Stapleton (Mike's gen-4 ancestor). He lived in Hawkins County, Tennessee in the early nineteenth century. He married Susan Singleton and together they raised at least eight children, including a son, Corporal William 'Buck' S. Stapleton, who would serve in the Civil War. The Stapletons were a prominent Lee Valley / Eidson family of the Hawkins County Cumberland Gap country.
