John Wilson Winstead

fl. early 1800s

Father of Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton. Of the Winstead family of Lee Valley, Hawkins County, Tennessee.

Documented

John Wilson Winstead (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Mary Fanny Lawson Winstead │ ▼ Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton (1841–1926)

John Wilson Winstead was the father of Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton (Mike's gen-4 ancestor). He lived at Lee Valley, Hawkins County, Tennessee in the early-to-mid nineteenth century. He married Mary Fanny Lawson and together they raised a large family of at least eleven children. The Winstead surname is English, well-attested in the Tennessee-Virginia Cumberland Gap country.
