Quesenberry Line — Generation 3

Eva Jane Slusher Quesenberry

1838–1926

Granddaughter of a Pennsylvania-German DAR Patriot, daughter of the Slushers and the Hyltons of Indian Valley, wife of one of the nine Confederate Quesenberrys. She lived from Andrew Jackson to Calvin Coolidge.

✓ Documented from public records

Eva Jane Slusher was born on the 20th of January, 1838 — the same year her father, Stephen Slusher, died, and the same year her grandmother Eve Hancock died. She came into the world at the precise moment that two generations of her family departed it.

Her surname, Slusher, is the anglicized form of the German Schlosser — meaning locksmith — and it traces to the great wave of Pennsylvania-Dutch and Palatine German settlers who poured down the Great Wagon Road into the Virginia and Carolina backcountry in the eighteenth century.

Her paternal grandfather was Christopher Slusher (1757–1845), a verified Revolutionary War soldier and DAR Patriot Ancestor #A105465, who served from Pennsylvania during the American Revolution before settling in Floyd County. Her paternal grandmother was Eve (Eve Jane) Hancock (1761–1838) — the original Eva Jane whose name Eva would carry. Her mother Charlotte Hylton (1804–1894) was a daughter of Archelaus Hylton and Catharine Weddle, of the great Hylton clan that had been among the very first white families to settle Indian Valley.

Eva Jane married William Henry Clay Quesenberry on the 15th of January, 1867 — the year of his twenty-fifth birthday and the second January after Appomattox. Together they raised six children. She survived him by twenty-eight years, dying on the 17th of December, 1926. She had lived through the presidencies of Andrew Jackson, Abraham Lincoln, and Calvin Coolidge.
