Harvey Line — Generation 8

James Stanley, Sr.

fl. early-mid 1700s

Husband of Catherine Hutchins Stanley. Together they raised eight children in colonial Virginia and migrated south to North Carolina.

Documented

James Stanley (fl. early-mid 1700s) ⚭ Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley │ ▼ Mary Agnes Stanley Hoggatt (1733–1808)

James Stanley, Sr. married Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins in Henrico, Virginia, on the 5th of March, 1728. Together they raised at least eight Stanley children, including Mary Agnes Stanley (later Mary Agnes Hoggatt — Mike's gen-7 ancestor). The Stanley family migrated south from Virginia to Guilford County, NC sometime in the mid-eighteenth century with the broader Quaker emigration down the Great Wagon Road. James's own birth and death dates are not yet documented.
