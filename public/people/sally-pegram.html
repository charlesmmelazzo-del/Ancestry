Mcmichael Line — Generation 3

Sally Pegram McMichael

1861–c.1895

Died young — between 1891 and 1897 — leaving Geneva (born 1891) motherless before she could remember her. The same Pegram-McMichael cycle of early-1890s loss that defined Geneva's childhood.

Documented

James Fletcher Pegram (1831–1915) Annie Elizabeth Gates Pegram (1826–1917) │ ▼ Sally Pegram McMichael (1861–c.1895) ⚭ William Franklin McMichael │ ▼ Geneva Esther McMicheal Harvey (1891–1971)

Sally Pegram was a daughter of James Fletcher Pegram (1831–1915) and Annie Elizabeth Gates (b. 1826) — both of the Piedmont North Carolina Pegram-Gates families. The Pegram surname is English (sometimes spelled Pilgrim in old records), well-attested in Piedmont North Carolina from the colonial period.

She married William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941) and bore him a daughter, Geneva Esther McMicheal, in 1891. (Geneva's middle name Esther may be a family name; whether from Sally's line or William's is yet to be determined.)
