Quesenberry Line — Generation 6

James S. Quesenberry

1796–1845

Born the year George Washington gave his Farewell Address, dead the year America declared war on Mexico. A southwest Virginia farmer.

Partially documented

James S. Quesenberry (1796–1845) ⚭ Elizabeth Moore Quesenberry │ ▼ Crockett Quisenberry (1822–1902)

James S. Quesenberry was born in 1796, the same year George Washington delivered his Farewell Address. He died in 1845, age forty-nine, in the year that the United States annexed Texas and provoked the war with Mexico. He married Elizabeth Moore (1800–1850), daughter of Peter Moore Sr., Pvt. — a man whose military rank suggests Revolutionary War or War of 1812 service. Their son Crockett would carry the line forward.
