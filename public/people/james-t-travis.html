Quesenberry Line — Generation 4

James T. Travis

fl. mid-late 1800s

Rebecca Jane Smyth's second husband. After her Confederate-soldier first husband was killed at Chancellorsville, she remarried Travis and bore at least five more children with him.

Partially documented

James T. Travis (sometimes recorded as James F. Travis) was the second husband of Rebecca Jane Smyth, after the Confederate war widowed her in 1863. With her he fathered at least five known children — James Edward, Robert Lee, Nannie J., Nancy Jane (later Garrett), and Frances M. — and possibly Sarah, Mattie, and Pelitha. The blended Quesenberry-Travis household lived in Lee County, Virginia. His Travis descendants are first cousins of Mike's direct line through Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr.

Travis and bore him several more children. Travis is not a direct ancestor of Mike's, but the Travis half-siblings of Alexander Henry Jr. represent an entire collateral branch of the family.
