Jane 'Jeany' Murtland Moore (fl. late 1700s) ⚭ Peter Moore Sr. │ ▼ Elizabeth Moore Quesenberry (1800–1850)

Jane Murtland — known by the diminutive Jeany — was the wife of Peter Moore Sr. Her surname Murtland is a Scots-Irish name from County Antrim and County Londonderry in Ulster, brought to America by the great Scots-Irish migrations of the 1700s. Her daughter Elizabeth Moore would marry into the Quesenberry line.
