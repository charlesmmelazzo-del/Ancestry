Harvey Line — Generation 2

William Carl Harvey

1887–1966

Ruth and Esther's father — first name was William, called Carl. Colfax farmer, Wesleyan churchman, husband of Geneva McMichael for forty-seven years.

Documented

Isaac J. Harvey (1842–1912) Rachel Frances Swift Harvey (fl. mid-1800s) │ ▼ William Carl Harvey (1887–1966) ⚭ Geneva Esther McMicheal Harvey │ ▼ Ruth Garland Harvey Quesenberry (1925–2016) Esther Harvey Scott (1929–2021)

The first thing to settle about Lonnie's father-in-law is the simplest question: what was his name? Ruth's 2016 obituary called him Carl; Esther's 2021 obituary called him Carol. The Ancestry records resolve it: his full given name was William Carl Harvey — born 1887, died 25 November 1966 in Greensboro. Carl was the name he was called by; William the formal first; Carol was a phonetic spelling variant on Esther's obituary.

He married Geneva Esther McMicheal on the 20th of June, 1919, in Guilford County, North Carolina. He was thirty-two; she was twenty-seven. They settled in the Deep River area of Guilford County, where the federal censuses of 1930 and 1950 record their household. Their daughter Ruth Garland Harvey was born in June 1925; their second daughter Esther arrived four years later in August 1929.

He was the son of Isaac J. Harvey and Rachel (Frances) Swift Harvey — both names confirmed in the genealogical record. The Harveys had been Piedmont North Carolina farmers for at least three generations.

Carl raised his daughters in the Wesleyan Church — Ruth at Greenwood Hills Wesleyan in High Point, Esther at Shady Grove Wesleyan in Colfax — an unusual denomination in the otherwise Methodist and Baptist Piedmont. He died on the 25th of November, 1966, in Greensboro, age 79. Geneva would survive him by five years.
