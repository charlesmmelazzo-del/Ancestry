Rachel Frances Swift Harvey

fl. mid-1800s

Mother of William Carl Harvey. Daughter of Thomas S. Swift and Lucinda Cox (1822–1889).

Documented

Thomas S. Swift (fl. early-mid 1800s) Lucinda Cox Swift (1822–1889) │ ▼ Rachel Frances Swift Harvey (fl. mid-1800s) ⚭ Isaac J. Harvey │ ▼ William Carl Harvey (1887–1966)

Rachel Frances Swift Harvey was a daughter of Thomas S. Swift and Lucinda Cox (1822–1889) of the Piedmont North Carolina Swift-Cox families. She married Isaac J. Harvey (1842–1912) and together they raised William Carl Harvey, who would become Ruth's father. The Swift surname is well-attested in Guilford and surrounding North Carolina counties through the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries.
