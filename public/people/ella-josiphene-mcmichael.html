Ella Josiphene McMichael Albert

fl. late 1800s

Geneva's older sister. Married into the Albert family.

Documented

William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941) Sally Pegram McMichael (1861–c.1895) │ ▼ Ella Josiphene McMichael Albert (fl. late 1800s)

Ella Josiphene McMichael was a daughter of William Franklin McMichael and Sally Pegram McMichael — and an older sister of Geneva (Ruth's mother). She later married a man named Albert and took that surname. The discovery of Ella and her brother Johnnie in May 2026 changed our understanding of Geneva's childhood: she was not an only child, but one of at least three siblings in a household where her mother died young (between 1891 and 1897).
