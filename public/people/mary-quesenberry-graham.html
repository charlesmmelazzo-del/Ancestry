Quesenberry Line — Generation 3

Mary E. Quesenberry Graham

fl. mid-late 1800s

The previously unknown sister of Alexander Henry Quesenberry Jr. — the daughter Rebecca was probably carrying when her husband died at Chancellorsville, or born shortly after.

Partially documented

Pvt. Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Sr. (1842–1863) Rebecca Jane Smyth Quesenberry Travis (1844–1920) │ ▼ Mary E. Quesenberry Graham (fl. mid-late 1800s)

Mary E. Quesenberry was a previously unknown daughter of Pvt. Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Sr. and Rebecca Jane Smyth — and sister of Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr. She would later marry into the Graham family. Whether she was born before or shortly after her father's death at Chancellorsville in May 1863 is not yet known. She is the eldest of Lonnie's grandfather's siblings on the Quesenberry side.
