Jesse Pegram

fl. early 1800s

Father of James Fletcher Pegram, grandfather of Sally, great-grandfather of Geneva. Of the Forsyth-Rockingham-Guilford Pegram family.

Partially documented

Jesse Pegram (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Lucretia Juanita Fuller Pegram │ ▼ James Fletcher Pegram (1831–1915)

Jesse Pegram — father of James Fletcher Pegram — was a member of the established Piedmont North Carolina Pegram family. He married Lucretia Juanita Fuller and together they raised at least four children: John Wyatt, Parthenia, Mary H., James Fletcher (b. 1831), and Jesse L. The Pegram surname is English, well-attested in Rockingham, Forsyth, and Guilford Counties throughout the nineteenth century.
