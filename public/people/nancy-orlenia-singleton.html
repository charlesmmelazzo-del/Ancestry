Nancy Orlenia Singleton Mullins

1815–1886

Wife of Hiram Mullins. Migrated west from Tennessee to Arkansas in her later years — died in Izard County, Arkansas.

Documented

Nancy Orlenia Singleton Mullins (1815–1886) ⚭ Hiram Mullins │ ▼ Harrison Mullins (1829–1920)

Nancy Orlenia Singleton was born in 1815 in Tennessee — the Singleton families were prominent in the southern Appalachians from the late eighteenth century forward. She married Hiram Mullins around 1828, when she was about thirteen and he was thirty-four — a substantial age gap typical of frontier remarriages. Together they raised Harrison Mullins (1829–1920) and likely other children.

After Hiram's 1850 death, Nancy lived another 36 years. She — or descendants who took her in — migrated west to Arkansas, where she died on the 13th of August 1886 in Izard County. Her great-great-grandson Lonnie would not be born for another 34 years.
