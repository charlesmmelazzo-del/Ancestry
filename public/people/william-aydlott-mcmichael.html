William Aydlott McMichael, Sr.

fl. late 1700s – early 1800s

Patriarch of the McMichaels of Guilford County. Husband of Rhoda Pegram — and thus the first generation to begin the Pegram-McMichael intermarriage that would define three generations of Deep River kinship.

Documented

William Aydlott McMichael (fl. late 1700s – early 1800s) ⚭ Rhoda E. Pegram McMichael │ ▼ Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907)

William Aydlott McMichael, Sr. — the founding patriarch of the McMichaels of Guilford County. He married Rhoda E. Pegram, beginning the first of what would become three generations of Pegram-McMichael intermarriage in the Deep River community. With Rhoda he raised at least eleven children: Jesse C. (Mike's gen-4 ancestor), Mary Ann (who later married a Pegram), Joseph Frank, Sarah H. (Highfill), Thomas A., Archibald McMichael (the real person whose name explains the long-running 'Archibald Carmichael' family memory), Nancy (also married a Pegram), Obadiah, John Watson, Charity, and William Aydelott Jr.

The unusual middle name Aydlott (sometimes Aydelott) was passed from father to son — a pattern that suggests it preserved an older family name from an earlier generation, possibly going back to a colonial Ulster-Scot or English progenitor whose surname has not yet been recovered.
