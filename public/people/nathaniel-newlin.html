Harvey Line — Generation 8

Nathaniel Newlin

1690–1732

Pennsylvania Quaker — son of an Irish Quaker emigrant who came to Penn's colony in 1683. The deepest Quaker-side ancestor on this site.

Documented

Nathaniel Newlin was born in 1690 in Chester County, Pennsylvania — a son of the Irish Quaker emigrant Nicholas Newlin (1630–1699), who had come over from Mountmellick, Queen's County, Ireland in 1683 with the wave of Quakers answering William Penn's invitation to settle the new colony of Pennsylvania.

Nathaniel married Jane Newlin (b. 1687) — note that the family practice of Friends often had wives keep their birth names, so it's also possible Jane was a cousin of Nathaniel's. Together they raised at least one daughter, Martha E. Newlin, whose own descendants would migrate south down the Great Wagon Road into the Carolina Piedmont in the eighteenth century, eventually merging into the Harvey line that descends to Ruth Garland Harvey.

Nathaniel died in 1732, age 42 — relatively young, even for that era. He is the deepest documented Quaker ancestor on this site.
