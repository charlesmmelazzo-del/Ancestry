Quesenberry Line — Generation 2

Eva Elizabeth Mullins Quesenberry

1890–1983

Maiden name Mullins — not Quesenberry. Lonnie's mother lived to the age of ninety-two, outliving her husband by thirteen years and her own century almost entire.

Documented

James Calloway Mullins (1854–1929) Annis Stapleton Mullins (fl. late 1800s) │ ▼ Eva Elizabeth Mullins Quesenberry (1890–1983) ⚭ Floyd Harmon Quesenberry │ ▼ Lonnie Olin Quesenberry (1920–2002) Billie R. Quesenberry (fl. 1940)

Eva Elizabeth Mullins was born in 1891, the daughter of James C. C. Mullins (most likely James Calaway "Cad/Cade" Mullins, 1854–1929) and Annis Stapleton. The Mullins surname is a famous one in southwestern Virginia and eastern Tennessee — perhaps most famous for the Melungeon Mullins families of Wise and Lee Counties, Virginia, though Eva's specific line has yet to be traced in detail.

She married Floyd Harmon Quesenberry before 1920 and bore him at least two sons — Lonnie Olin (1920) in Kingsport, Tennessee, and Billie R. Quesenberry. In the 1940 federal census the family is recorded at Western Branch, Norfolk, Virginia, and Eva appears as "Evvie" — the household name everyone called her by.

She lived to be ninety-two, dying in 1983. By then her son Lonnie had been married thirty-five years and her grandchildren were grown.
