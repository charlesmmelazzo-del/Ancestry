Quesenberry Line — Generation 2

Eva Quesenberry

fl. 1920s

A mother whose first name echoes through five generations of Quesenberry women — Eva, Eve, Eva Jane.

Not yet documented

Eva Quesenberry (fl. 1920s) ⚭ Floyd H. Quesenberry │ ▼ Lonnie Olin Quesenberry (1920–2002)

Of Eva Quesenberry, even less is presently known than of her husband. She was Lonnie's mother. Her maiden name has not yet surfaced in the public record — Find a Grave lists her under her married surname, which raises the possibility (common in these heavily intermarried Appalachian families) that she was herself born a Quesenberry, a cousin or second cousin of Floyd's. It is equally possible the indexer simply did not record her birth name.

What is striking is the persistence of the name Eva in this family. Floyd's mother (or grandmother) was very probably Eva Jane Slusher Quesenberry (1838–1926), wife of William Henry Quesenberry. Before her, in colonial Virginia, was an Eve Hancock (1761–1838), wife of Christopher Schlosser/Slusher. The name carried down the generations — from a colonial Eve in the Tidewater, to an Eva Jane in the Floyd County hollows, to an Eva at the dawn of the twentieth century, who in turn named no daughter Eva but gave her son Lonnie an O name (Olin) that would echo through the Quesenberrys to come.
