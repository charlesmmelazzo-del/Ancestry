Harvey Line — Generation 8

Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley

1703–1766

Born Henrico, Virginia 1703. Married James Stanley 1728. Buried at New Garden Friends Meeting in Guilford NC, 1766. The deepest Hutchins-Stanley ancestor on the Harvey side.

Documented

Nicholas Hutchins (fl. late 1600s – early 1700s) Mary Watkins Hutchins (fl. late 1600s – early 1700s) │ ▼ Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins Stanley (1703–1766) ⚭ James Stanley, Sr. │ ▼ Mary Agnes Stanley Hoggatt (1733–1808)

Catherine "Rachel" Hutchins was born around 1703 in Henrico, Virginia, British Colonial America — to Nicholas Hutchins and Mary Watkins. She grew up in a large family of at least eleven siblings: Strangeman, Martha, Mary (later Harris), James, Carrie (later McNeal), John, Sarah, Benjamin, Elizabeth, Alice, and herself.

On the 5th of March, 1728, in Henrico, Virginia, she married James Stanley, Sr. Over the next two-plus decades they raised at least eight Stanley children: William, James Jr., Mary Agnes (Mike's gen-7 ancestor — who would later marry Anthony P. Hoggatt), Martha (later Thornburgh), Agnes (later Jessup), Strangeman (named for Catherine's brother), Elijah, and Micajah.

Sometime in the mid-eighteenth century the Stanley family migrated south from Virginia to North Carolina, joining the broader Quaker movement down the Great Wagon Road into the Piedmont. Catherine died on the 20th of December, 1766, in Guilford County, NC, and was buried at New Garden Friends Monthly Meeting — one of the foundational Quaker meetings of the Carolina Quaker community. New Garden would, over the next century, become the heart of the Friends presence in Piedmont NC and a center of antislavery sentiment.

Catherine is the deepest Hutchins-Stanley ancestor we have documented. Through her daughter Mary Agnes (later Hoggatt) the Stanley line merges into the Hoggatt-Newlin-Harvey lineage that leads down to Ruth Garland Harvey six generations later.
