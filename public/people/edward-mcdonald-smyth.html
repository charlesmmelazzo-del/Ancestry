Edward McDonald Smyth (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ Minerva Parsons Smyth │ ▼ Rebecca Jane Smyth Quesenberry Travis (1844–1920)

Edward McDonald Smyth — sometimes spelled Smythe — was the father of Rebecca Jane Smyth Quesenberry Travis, born in Lee County, Virginia in 1844. He married Minerva Parsons and together they raised at least nine children in the Cumberland Gap country of southwestern Virginia. The Smyth/Smith surname was common across the Scots-Irish Appalachian highlands.
