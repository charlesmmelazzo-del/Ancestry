Elizabeth Moore Quesenberry

1800–1850

Born with the new century, daughter of Peter Moore Sr., Pvt.

Partially documented

Peter Moore Sr. (fl. late 1700s) Jane 'Jeany' Murtland Moore (fl. late 1700s) │ ▼ Elizabeth Moore Quesenberry (1800–1850) ⚭ James S. Quesenberry │ ▼ Crockett Quisenberry (1822–1902)

Elizabeth Moore was born in 1800, daughter of Peter Moore Sr., Pvt. and Jane Jeany Murtland. The 'Pvt.' in her father's recorded name marks him as a military veteran — almost certainly of the War of 1812, possibly of the American Revolution. Elizabeth's marriage to James S. Quesenberry brought the Moore line into the Quesenberry tree.
