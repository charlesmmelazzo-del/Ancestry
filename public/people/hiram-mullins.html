Quesenberry Line — Generation 5

Hiram Mullins

1794–1850

Father of Harrison Mullins. Born in North Carolina under George Washington's second term; died in Tennessee just before the Civil War.

Documented

Hiram Mullins (1794–1850) ⚭ Nancy Orlenia Singleton Mullins │ ▼ Harrison Mullins (1829–1920)

Hiram Mullins was born in 1794 in North Carolina — likely western NC or the Tennessee borderlands, where the Mullins families were part of the great westward migration of the late eighteenth century. He moved (or his family moved) into Tennessee, where he married Nancy Orlenia Singleton (1815–1886) — twenty-one years his junior. Their son Harrison Mullins would carry the line forward into the next generation. Hiram died in 1850, age 56, just over a decade before the Civil War would change everything.
