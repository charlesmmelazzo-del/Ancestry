Harvey Line — Generation 5

Mary Outland Trueblood

fl. late 1700s – early 1800s

Mother of Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey. Of the colonial Quaker Outland family of North Carolina.

Partially documented

Mary Outland Trueblood (fl. late 1700s – early 1800s) ⚭ Isaac Trueblood │ ▼ Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey (1812–before 1870)

Mary Outland married Isaac Trueblood in late eighteenth or early nineteenth-century North Carolina. She is named in her daughter Sarah Sally's record on Geni World Family Tree as 'Mary Trueblood (born Outland)' — confirming her maiden name. Together with Isaac she raised at least nine Trueblood children. The Outland surname is, like Trueblood, a colonial English Quaker name well-attested in the Perquimans-Pasquotank area of NC's Albemarle Sound region from the 1670s forward — the two families were neighbors in that earliest Carolina Quaker community for generations before both lines migrated inland to Guilford.
