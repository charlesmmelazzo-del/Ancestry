James Calloway Mullins

1854–1929

Born March 1854 in Hancock County, Tennessee; died 1929 in Lee County, Virginia. Father of Eva Mullins. Of the great Mullins clan of the Cumberland Gap.

Documented

James Calloway Mullins was born in March 1854 in Hancock County, Tennessee — the heart of the Cumberland Gap country, where Tennessee, Kentucky, and Virginia meet. He was the son of Harrison Mullins (1829–1920) and Manerva Jane Mullins (1831–1910) — Mullinses on both sides, suggesting cousin marriage common in the clan.

The Mullins surname is one of the most studied in Appalachia, in part because of the Melungeon families of Wise, Lee, Hancock, and Hawkins Counties — a tri-racial isolate community whose origins remain debated. Whether James Calloway's specific line is part of the documented Melungeon Mullinses or a parallel branch is a research question worth pursuing.

He married Annis Stapleton (1856–1934, of Hawkins County, TN) and together they raised Eva Elizabeth Mullins (1890–1983) — Lonnie's mother. He died on the 10th of November, 1929, at the Yokum Station District of Lee County, Virginia, age 75 — having crossed the state line from Tennessee in his later years.
