Floyd Harmon Quesenberry (1884–1970) Eva Elizabeth Mullins Quesenberry (1890–1983) │ ▼ Billie R. Quesenberry (fl. 1940)

Billie R. Quesenberry appears in the 1940 federal census household at Western Branch, Norfolk, Virginia, alongside Lonnie and their parents Floyd and 'Evvie' Quesenberry. The middle initial R. and the unisex name Billie leave open whether this was a brother or a sister. The next research step is to pull the 1930 census and locate Billie's birth and death records — once known, Billie's descendants may still be living, and represent an entire collateral branch of the family not yet on this site.
