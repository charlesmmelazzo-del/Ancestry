Billie Ray Quesenberry

fl. 1920s–?

Documented

Floyd Harmon Quesenberry (1884–1970) Eva Elizabeth Mullins Quesenberry (1890–1983) │ ▼ Billie Ray Quesenberry (fl. 1920s–?)

Billie Ray Quesenberry was a son of Floyd Harmon Quesenberry and Eva Elizabeth Mullins, and one of Lonnie's brothers. He appears in the 1940 federal census household at Western Branch, Norfolk, Virginia, alongside Lonnie and their parents Floyd and 'Evvie' Quesenberry — listed as 'Billie R. Quesenberry'. The MyHeritage record-match deep dive of May 2026 resolved the middle initial: his full name was Billie Ray Quesenberry, and he was male. Birth and death dates and any descendants still await further research.
