Matilda Jane Price Quesenberry

1867–1944

Wife of Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr.; mother of Floyd. Daughter of Alexander Stewart Price (1833–1909). Lived from Reconstruction through the Second World War.

Documented

Alexander Stewart Price (1833–1909) Martha Jane Collins Price (fl. mid-1800s) │ ▼ Matilda Jane Price Quesenberry (1867–1944) ⚭ Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr. │ ▼ Floyd Harmon Quesenberry (1884–1970)

Matilda Jane Price was born in 1867, two years after Appomattox, daughter of Alexander Stewart Price (1833–1909) and Martha Jane Collins. She married Alexander Henry Quesenberry, Jr. — son of the Civil War widow Rebecca Smyth — and together they raised at least one son, Floyd Harmon Quesenberry, who would carry the family south to Tennessee.

She lived to be seventy-seven, dying in 1944 — by which time her grandson Lonnie was twenty-four and would soon return from the Norfolk shipyards to begin his married life in Greensboro.
