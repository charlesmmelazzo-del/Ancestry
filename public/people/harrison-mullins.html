Quesenberry Line — Generation 4

Harrison Mullins

1829–1920

Father of James Calloway Mullins. Lived ninety-one years from Andrew Jackson to Warren G. Harding; saw his son grow old.

Documented

Hiram Mullins (1794–1850) Nancy Orlenia Singleton Mullins (1815–1886) │ ▼ Harrison Mullins (1829–1920) ⚭ Manerva Jane Mullins │ ▼ James Calloway Mullins (1854–1929)

Harrison Mullins was born in 1829 in Tennessee — likely Hancock or Claiborne County, the Mullins heartland of the Tennessee mountains. His parents were Hiram Mullins (1794–1850), a North Carolina-born Mullins who had come west to Tennessee, and Nancy Orlenia Singleton (1815–1886), of the Tennessee Singletons.

He married Manerva Jane Mullins — note the shared surname, common in the heavily intermarried Mullins families of the Cumberland Gap country, suggesting they were cousins. Together they raised at least one son, James Calloway Mullins (1854–1929) — Eva Mullins' father.

Harrison appears to have lived through more than one marriage, since records show James Calloway had several younger half-siblings (Henry, Jacob F., Wiley Hillery, John D., Mary E.) of slightly later birth years. Harrison lived an extraordinary 91 years, dying in 1920 in Dickenson County, Virginia — by which time his great-great-grandson Lonnie had just been born in nearby Sullivan County, Tennessee.
