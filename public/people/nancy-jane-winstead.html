Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton

1841–?

Wife of Robert Thomas Stapleton, mother of Annis. Born the year of William Henry Harrison's brief presidency.

Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton (1841–?) ⚭ Robert Thomas Stapleton │ ▼ Annis Stapleton Mullins (fl. late 1800s)

Nancy Jane Winstead was born in 1841 — the year of William Henry Harrison's brief one-month presidency. She married Robert Thomas Stapleton, eleven years her senior, and bore Annis Stapleton — who would marry James C. C. Mullins and become Eva Mullins' mother.
