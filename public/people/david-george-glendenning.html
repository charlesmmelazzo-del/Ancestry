David George Glendenning

fl. late 1600s – early 1700s

Edinburgh-born father of Mary Glendenning Hoggatt. The deepest known Scottish ancestor in this tree.

Partially documented

David George Glendenning (fl. late 1600s – early 1700s) ⚭ Margaret Hall Glendenning │ ▼ Mary Glendenning Hoggatt (1698–1780)

David George Glendenning was a resident of Edinburgh, Midlothian, Scotland in the late seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries. He married Margaret Hall and together they raised at least five children: Agnes, Walter, Isabell, John, and Mary (born 8 December 1698 — Mike's gen-8 direct ancestor who would emigrate to America and marry Philip Hoggatt around 1720).

The Glendenning surname is Scottish, specifically associated with the Border country between Scotland and England — the Glendennings of Drumrash and Parton in Galloway are the best-documented branch, but the name had also spread to Edinburgh by the 1600s. David George Glendenning is the deepest known Scottish ancestor in the entire tree, putting Mike's documented ancestry firmly in seventeenth-century Edinburgh.
