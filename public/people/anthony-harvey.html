Anthony Harvey (fl. early-mid 1800s) ⚭ Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey │ ▼ Isaac J. Harvey (1842–1912)

Anthony Harvey — father of Isaac J. Harvey. He married Sarah Sally Trueblood, of the colonial Quaker Trueblood family of Virginia and North Carolina. The Trueblood surname (English Quaker) connects this Harvey branch back to the Quaker migrations from Pennsylvania and Maryland into the Carolina Piedmont in the eighteenth century — a connection further confirmed by the deeper colonial Newlin and Hoggatt ancestors documented elsewhere in this site.
