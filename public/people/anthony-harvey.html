Harvey Line — Generation 4

Anthony Harvey

1791–1846

Born April 23, 1791, Alamance County NC. Lived 55 years. Father of Isaac J. Harvey, son of Nathan Heaver Harvey and Agnes Harvey.

Documented

Nathan Heaver Harvey (1759–1837) Agnes Harvey (1756–1834) │ ▼ Anthony Harvey (1791–1846) ⚭ Sarah Sally Trueblood Harvey │ ▼ Isaac J. Harvey (1842–1912)

Anthony Harvey was born on the 23rd of April, 1791, in Alamance County, North Carolina (which was then still part of Orange County — Alamance would not be carved out until 1849). He was a son of Nathan Heaver Harvey (1759–1837) and Agnes Harvey (1756–1834), both of the Pennsylvania-then-North-Carolina Quaker Harveys. Anthony had five known siblings: Mary, Isaac Harvey (1793–1848), Moses, Elizabeth, and Phebe.

He married Sarah Sally Trueblood (1812–before 1870) — born on the 21st of September, 1812, in Guilford County, NC, daughter of Isaac Trueblood and Mary Outland Trueblood, of the colonial Quaker Trueblood family. Together they raised Isaac J. Harvey (1842–1912), born when Anthony was already 51 years old — likely a late-life child.

Anthony died in 1846, age 55. His widow Sarah later migrated west to Missouri, where she died before 1870, leaving Isaac J. (then in his late twenties) to remain in North Carolina and start the family line that descends to Ruth.
