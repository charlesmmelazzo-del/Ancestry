Jane Newlin

1687–?

Pennsylvania Quaker. Wife of Nathaniel Newlin.

Documented

Jane Newlin was born in 1687 — three years older than her husband Nathaniel. She was part of the colonial Pennsylvania Quaker community gathered in Chester County under William Penn's holy experiment. From her line descend the Quaker-rooted Harveys of Piedmont North Carolina.
