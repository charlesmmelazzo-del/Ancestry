Susan Singleton Stapleton

fl. early 1800s

Mother of Robert Thomas Stapleton. Maiden name Singleton — the same surname found two generations later in Nancy Orlenia Singleton (Hiram Mullins's wife).

Documented

Susan Singleton Stapleton (fl. early 1800s) ⚭ James Franklin Stapleton │ ▼ Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911)

Susan Singleton married James Franklin Stapleton in early nineteenth-century Hawkins County, Tennessee. Together they raised at least eight Stapleton children, including Robert Thomas Stapleton (born 1830). Her maiden name Singleton is notable: it recurs elsewhere in Lonnie's maternal tree — Nancy Orlenia Singleton married Hiram Mullins in the same Cumberland Gap region. The Singletons, Stapletons, Mullinses, and Winsteads of Hawkins and Claiborne Counties formed a dense, intermarried Appalachian kin-network across several generations.
