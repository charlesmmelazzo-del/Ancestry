Harvey Line — Generation 1

Esther Harvey Scott

1929–2021

Ruth's younger sister — born in Colfax, married just before Ruth, served as a private-duty and office nurse in Kernersville for most of her life.

✓ Documented from public records

Esther Harvey Scott was born on the 2nd of August, 1929, in Colfax, the small farming community of western Guilford County, NC, to Carol and Geneva Harvey — Ruth Garland Harvey's parents. She was Ruth's younger sister by four years and one month.

On the 9th of August 1947 — about eleven months before Ruth's own wedding — Esther married Anderson L. Scott. They settled in Kernersville, in adjacent Forsyth County. Esther trained as a Licensed Practical Nurse and spent her working life caring for patients: as a private-duty nurse in homes around Kernersville, then on the wards of Forsyth Memorial Hospital, and finally as office nurse for Dr. Leo Record in Kernersville. She remained a homemaker through it all.

Like her sister, she carried her parents' Wesleyan faith into her adult life. Where Ruth attended Greenwood Hills Wesleyan Church in High Point, Esther worshiped at Shady Grove Wesleyan Church back in her birthplace of Colfax — and there, in the Shady Grove churchyard, she was buried after several months of decline, on the 3rd of February 2021. She had outlived her older sister by five years.

Esther's published obituary is, on the present record, the single most important documentary source for the Harvey side of this family — it is what gave us her parents' first names, her birthplace, her birth date, her wedding date and husband, and her burial place. Without it, the Harvey-Carmichael line would still be no more than the four words "Carl Harvey and Geneva Carmichael Harvey" from Ruth's own obituary.
