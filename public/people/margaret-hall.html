Margaret Hall Glendenning

fl. late 1600s

Edinburgh-born mother of Mary Glendenning Hoggatt.

Partially documented

Margaret Hall Glendenning (fl. late 1600s) ⚭ David George Glendenning │ ▼ Mary Glendenning Hoggatt (1698–1780)

Margaret Hall married David George Glendenning in Edinburgh, Scotland in the late 1600s. Together they raised Mary Glendenning, who would emigrate to colonial America and marry Philip Hoggatt around 1720. The Hall surname is broadly English/Scottish, common across the British Isles. Margaret is the deepest documented matriarch on the Hoggatt-Glendenning side of the tree.
