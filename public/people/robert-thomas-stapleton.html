Robert Thomas Stapleton

1830–1911

Father of Annis Stapleton, grandfather of Eva Mullins. A Lee Valley, Tennessee man who lived eighty-one years; a photograph of him survives.

Documented

James Franklin Stapleton (fl. early 1800s) Susan Singleton Stapleton (fl. early 1800s) │ ▼ Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911) ⚭ Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton │ ▼ Annis Stapleton Mullins (fl. late 1800s)

Robert Thomas Stapleton was born on the 28th of March, 1830, at Lee Valley, Hawkins County, Tennessee — to James Franklin Stapleton and Susan Singleton Stapleton. He had at least seven siblings, including a brother, Corporal William 'Buck' S. Stapleton, who served in the Civil War. (The Singleton maiden name of his mother echoes through this family — Nancy Orlenia Singleton married Hiram Mullins on the other side of Eva's tree; the Singletons, Stapletons, and Mullinses were all Cumberland Gap families who intermarried repeatedly.)

He married Nancy Jane Winstead (1841–1926) — eleven years younger than him — and together they raised at least eight children, including Annis (1856–1934), who would marry James Calloway Mullins. Their granddaughter Eva Elizabeth Mullins (1890–1983) would become Lonnie's mother.

Robert Thomas lived to eighty, dying on the 25th of February, 1911, at Eidson, Hawkins County, Tennessee — in the same Lee Valley country where he was born. A photograph of him survives among the MyHeritage record matches.
