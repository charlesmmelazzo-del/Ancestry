Robert Thomas Stapleton

1830–1911

Father of Annis Stapleton, grandfather of Eva Mullins. Lived eighty-one years through the Civil War, Reconstruction, and the dawn of the automobile age.

Documented

Robert Thomas Stapleton (1830–1911) ⚭ Nancy Jane Winstead Stapleton │ ▼ Annis Stapleton Mullins (fl. late 1800s)

Robert Thomas Stapleton was born in 1830 — the year before Floyd County, Virginia was formed. He married Nancy Jane Winstead (b. 1841) — eleven years younger than him — and together they raised Annis Stapleton, who would marry James C. C. Mullins. Their granddaughter Eva Elizabeth Mullins (1891–1983) would become Lonnie's mother. Robert Thomas lived to eighty-one, dying in 1911.
