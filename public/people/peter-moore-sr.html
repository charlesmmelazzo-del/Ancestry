Military Service Recorded as 'Pvt.' — likely Revolutionary War or War of 1812 service

Peter Moore Sr. is preserved in the family record with the title Pvt. — a private in the United States military. The most likely possibilities are American Revolutionary War service (if he was born in the 1740s–1760s) or War of 1812 service (if born in the 1770s–1790s). Either would mark him as a Patriot ancestor eligible for DAR / SAR membership.

He married Jane 'Jeany' Murtland — a Scottish or Scots-Irish name pointing to the Highland or Ulster-Scot migrations of the eighteenth century. Their daughter Elizabeth Moore married James S. Quesenberry, joining the Moore line into the Quesenberry tree.

Resolving Peter Moore Sr.'s service record is a high-priority open question. Pension files at the National Archives, the DAR Patriot Index, and the SAR application files in Washington, D.C. are the places to look.
