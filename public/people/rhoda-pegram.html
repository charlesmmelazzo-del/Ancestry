Mcmichael Line — Generation 5

Rhoda E. Pegram McMichael

fl. late 1700s – early 1800s

The bridge between the Pegram and McMichael families. Her marriage to William Aydlott McMichael Sr. began three generations of intermarriage in Deep River.

Documented

Rhoda E. Pegram McMichael (fl. late 1700s – early 1800s) ⚭ William Aydlott McMichael, Sr. │ ▼ Jesse C. McMichael (1820–1907)

Rhoda E. Pegram — daughter of an earlier-generation Pegram (parents not yet identified) — married William Aydlott McMichael, Sr. in Guilford County, North Carolina sometime around 1810–1815. Their son Jesse C. McMichael (b. 1820) would carry both family names forward, raising his own children in Deep River where the Pegrams and McMichaels lived side by side.

Rhoda is structurally one of the most important ancestors on the McMichael side. Through her, the Pegram surname enters this branch two generations earlier than through Sally Pegram (Geneva's mother). That means Geneva McMichael Harvey is descended from Pegrams along two distinct lines — Rhoda her great-great-grandmother (paternal), and Sally her mother. The Pegrams and McMichaels of Deep River were not merely neighbors who occasionally intermarried; they were a single extended kin-network whose descendants are often related two or three different ways.
