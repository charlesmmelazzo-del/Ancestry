Four Centuries

A Family Timeline

From Jamestown to High Point — the family story set against the spine of American history.

  1. 1607Jamestown founded — the English colony of Virginia opens for business.
  2. 1608Thomas Questenbury born in Bromley, Kent, England.
  3. 1624The Atlantic Crossing. Thomas Questenbury, age 16, arrives in Virginia.
  4. 1627John Quisenberry born in colonial Virginia — the first American-born of the line.
  5. 1672Thomas Questenbury dies in Canterbury, having returned to England in old age.
  6. 1717John Quisenberry dies in Westmoreland County, Virginia, at about age 90.
  7. 1725Aaron Quisenberry Sr. born, Caroline County, Virginia.
  8. 1741Aaron Quisenberry, age 16, marries Joyce Gayle Dudley in King George County.
  9. 1748George Quesenberry born in Orange County, Virginia.
  10. 1754Highland Birth. Archibald Carmichael born in Lanark, Scotland — exactly 21 years before he sails for America.
  11. 1775–1781American Revolution. George Quesenberry serves with the Montgomery County, Virginia Militia. Aaron Quisenberry Sr. furnishes beef from his Orange County farm to the Continental Army — an act for which he is later listed as a DAR & SAR Patriot Ancestor.
  12. 4 Sep 1775The Second Atlantic Crossing. On his 21st birthday, Archibald Carmichael sails from Larne aboard the Jupiter of Larne for Wilmington, North Carolina.
  13. 1795Aaron Quisenberry Sr. dies in St. Thomas Parish, Orange County, Virginia.
  14. 1812George Quesenberry dies in Floyd County, Virginia, in the year America declares its second war on Britain.
  15. 1827Archibald Carmichael dies in Forsyth County, North Carolina.
  16. 1831Floyd County, Virginia is formed from Montgomery County.
  17. 1838Eva Jane Slusher born in Floyd County, Virginia.
  18. 1841William Henry Quesenberry born in Indian Valley, Floyd County, Virginia.
  19. 1853John Quesenberry Sr. dies in Wythe County, Virginia.
  20. 1861–1865The Civil War. Multiple Quesenberry men of Floyd County serve in the Confederate Army — including Frederick Quesenberry of the 54th Virginia Infantry. Family tradition holds that nine sons of John Quesenberry Sr.'s line served in all.
  21. 1867William Henry Quesenberry marries Eva Jane Slusher — barely two years after Appomattox.
  22. 1898William Henry Quesenberry dies in Glendale, Arizona Territory — having migrated west in old age.
  23. 1920Lonnie Olin Quesenberry born in North Carolina.
  24. 1925Ruth Garland Harvey born in Guilford County, North Carolina.
  25. 1926Eva Jane Slusher Quesenberry dies, age 88.
  26. 1941–1945World War II. Lonnie Quesenberry comes of military age.
  27. 17 July 1948The Marriage. Lonnie marries Ruth in Greensboro, NC.
  28. 2002Lonnie Olin Quesenberry dies, age 82, at High Point, NC.
  29. 2016Ruth Garland Harvey Quesenberry dies, age 90, at High Point, NC. Three centuries and four months separate her from the boy who stepped off the ship in Virginia.
