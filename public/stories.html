1955 · High Point, NC

Ruth's Sunday Pound Cake

As told by Family example · recorded EXAMPLE

About: Ruth Garland Harvey Quesenberry

EXAMPLE ENTRY — replace with a real story.

Ruth was, the obituary said, an excellent self-taught cook. Her Sunday pound cake was famous for using a full pound of butter and a full pound of sugar, beaten by hand for at least thirty minutes — she insisted electric mixers ruined the texture.

She served it warm with no glaze, only a dusting of powdered sugar, and she expected the children to eat the slice she gave them and not ask for seconds — though they always did.
