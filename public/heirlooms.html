Ruth's Sunday Pound Cake

From: Ruth Garland Harvey Quesenberry

EXAMPLE ENTRY — replace with the real handwritten recipe card if it survives. The classic Southern 1-1-1 pound cake (pound of butter, pound of sugar, pound of flour, plus eggs and flavoring), beaten by hand. No glaze, only powdered sugar.

Ingredients

1 pound (4 sticks) salted butter, room temperature

1 pound (about 2¼ cups) granulated sugar

9 large eggs

1 pound (about 4 cups) plain all-purpose flour, sifted

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Method

Butter and flour a tube pan. Cream butter and sugar by hand for at least 20 minutes until very light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each. Fold in flour by thirds. Add vanilla. Pour into pan, place in a cold oven, then turn oven to 325°F. Bake 1 hour 15 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in pan 30 minutes before turning out. Dust with powdered sugar — never glaze.
