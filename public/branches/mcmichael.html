A Family Line

The McMichael Line

A Scots-Irish name in the Deep River farms of Guilford County, North Carolina

McMichael (occasionally spelled McMicheal) is a Scots-Irish surname carried to America from Ulster in the eighteenth century. It is sometimes confused with the Highland Scottish surname Carmichael — and earlier research on this site mistakenly traced Geneva's line back to Archibald Carmichael of Lanarkshire. That line has now been retracted: Mike's Ancestry tree confirms the maiden name as McMichael. Geneva Esther McMicheal Harvey (1891–1971) was a daughter of William Franklin McMichael (1849–1941) of Deep River, Guilford County, North Carolina — and the McMichael line traces back through Piedmont North Carolina, with roots almost certainly in the Scots-Irish migrations down the Great Wagon Road from Pennsylvania in the eighteenth century.
