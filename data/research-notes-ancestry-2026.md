## Strategic note
The Tennessee birthplace matters: Sullivan County, Tennessee is in the far NE of TN,
abuts Wise/Lee Counties of Virginia. This is consistent with a Quesenberry migration
*south and west* from Floyd County VA → far SW Virginia → NE Tennessee, then back EAST
to Norfolk for industrial/naval work, then SOUTH to NC. Floyd Harmon Quesenberry
(1885–1970) likely lived in TN at the time of his marriage to Eva Mullins.
